# Polmarex-Business-ERP-

Premier Polmarex Inc. Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) & AI Operations Platform for B2B Wholesale (`premierpolmarex.ca`), B2C Retail (`maplemart.com`), and Brand Direct (`nutrogusto.com`).

## Modules Included
- **Executive Overview**: Live revenue, order volume, customer retention, and inventory health KPIs.
- **Lead Management**: B2B & B2C pipeline across all 3 storefronts with automated AI lead scoring & Kanban pipeline.
- **Customers / CRM**: 360-degree account view, inactivity segmentation (`At risk` after 45d, `Inactive` after 90d), Excel import/export, and AI Personalized Follow-Up generator.
- **Sales Reps Dashboard**: Individual representative productivity, brand performance matrix, missed targets, overdue receivables, and AI-optimized weekly route plans.
- **Inventory & Forecasting**: Multi-warehouse stock monitoring, days-of-cover tracking, and reorder point alerts.
- **Orders & Receivables**: Full wholesale order management and AR aging (`Current`, `1–30d`, `31–60d`, `61–90d`, `90d+`).
- **AI Agents Centre**: 4 specialized AI agents (`Customer Retention`, `Inventory Forecasting`, `Lead Generation`, `Sales Intelligence`) with Human-in-the-Loop approval queue.
- **Light & Dark Theme**: Full support for Light Mode and Obsidian-Slate Dark Mode.
